# Prospective LinkedIn voice profile v2

The owner asked for future posts to follow the plain-language tone of their
merged example. This is **prompt conditioning plus offline regression checks**,
not model fine-tuning, a humanizer score or evidence that live generated quality
has improved. The claimed external checker scores were not verified or adopted.

## Writing contract

For the existing **standard LinkedIn post** format, public copy targets 900–1300
Unicode characters, including spaces and line breaks but excluding the platform
heading. The legacy `shortform`/`longform` planning label does not replace that
post target. This does not change the weekly cadence or introduce article
publishing. A future genuine article mode needs its own explicit format and
range; the post range is not an article word-count requirement.

The voice uses plain words, minimal necessary jargon, a source-backed problem,
brief project context, concrete change and qualified observed result. It varies
sentence lengths, uses natural contractions and straight quotes/apostrophes,
and avoids em dashes. Material uncertainty belongs in one or two plain sentences
before a useful closing lesson or a focused reflective question grounded in the
episode, not a long catalogue of technical exclusions. Specific useful questions
are welcome; generic engagement bait is not. Required limits
and evidence still take priority; missing evidence is an editorial hold, not an
excuse to pad or invent a personal experience.

Vocabulary substitutions are meaning-preserving examples only. No post, sample
number, wake/sleep claim, computing-hour conversion or reported score is a golden
factual example. Ownership, directing agents, observations and feelings require
cleared source support or separately validated task-bound owner context. This
patch adds no factual context, new clearance or arbitrary-source identity grant.

## Roles and observations

Story Miner receives a compact style-aware selection instruction; Strategist
plans within its existing schema, including canonical IDs/labels rather than
new prose when applicable. Writer and Adapter apply the full profile. Critic
evaluates rather than writing a replacement post. All six provider schemas,
privacy checks, source bindings, budget caps and exact approval gates stay intact.

Only a **new Critic prompt** receives deterministic observations of the exact
current Writer/Adapter bytes: character count, curly quotes, em dashes and a
bounded list of sentence word counts. Mechanical violations ask the Critic to
use its existing editorial-hold field, never an invented factual verdict.
Transparent review hints flag three or more distinct technical terms, audit-like
framing and a disclaimer or an exact generic engagement-bait ending. A specific reflective
question is not flagged merely for ending in a question mark. These are advisory review prompts:
a necessary technical term is not automatically wrong, a hint is not a semantic
verdict, and an empty list is never a style or factual pass. Saved outputs are
not rewritten or trimmed. This is not a new deterministic publishing-approval
bypass or an automatic quality certification.

## Versioning and immutable history

The current profile is `owner-style-v2-2026-10-26`; its hash covers its guidance.
New prompt receipts bind that profile and the exact rendered prompt. Stored v1
receipts remain readable as v1 and cannot be relabeled as new executions. No
historical Critic result, saved review decision or comparison artifact is
retroactively regraded by these diagnostics.

The current canonical-LF prompt-policy manifest is regenerated. The consumed
corrected-comparison scope, its consent and its pinned historical policy digest
remain unchanged. New policy drift deliberately blocks that scope from another
execution while preserving historical verification and provenance. This patch
creates no new comparison scope, retry, model call, credential or scheduler
change, deployment, content approval or publication.

## Verification limits

Tests render all six stages with the exact cleared source and saved predecessor
artifacts, check both normal-production and historical comparison bounds, test
mechanical boundaries and advisory hints, preserve source-grounding behavior,
and verify old permission cannot authorize the changed policy. These are offline
checks, not a paid generation or proof every future post will meet the voice.
The independent source-bound owner-context feature is separate from this patch.
